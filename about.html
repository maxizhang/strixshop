About Strix Shop

Strix Shop was created to make essential items for students more affordable, reliable, and accessible.

Overview

What is Strix Shop?

Strix Shop is a student-run local e-commerce platform built to make essential items, such as aesthetic stationery, keychains, accessories, toys, games, and more products more affordable for students.

Mission

Our Goal

Our mission is to bring products with great quality and affordability, ensuring every student has seamless access to the tools they need to thrive.

Community

Built by Students

Every part of Strix Shop is engineered, designed, and managed directly by students. We understand the daily academic environment, enabling us to better choose our selection of inventory and coordinate delivery.
