Returns & Refunds Policy

Version 1 | Effective August 1st, 2026

1. Overview & Initiation

Strix Shop strives to ensure you are fully satisfied with your purchases. If you need to request a return or a refund, you must contact our Customer Support team directly to review your case and initiate the process. Unauthorised or direct returns without prior support approval may not be accepted.

2. Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return or exchange, items must meet the following criteria:

- Requests must be submitted within 14 days of delivery or order receipt.
- Items must be unused, unworn (if applicable), and in their original packaging with all tags attached.
- Proof of purchase or order confirmation number must be provided when contacting support.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Certain types of goods cannot be returned or refunded, including:

- Customised or personalised products made specifically to order.
- Perishable goods, personal care items, or digital goods.
- Final sale or clearance items marked down at the time of purchase.

4. Damaged or Defective Items

If you received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please notify Customer Support immediately upon arrival. Include clear photos of the damage or issue along with your order details so our team can swiftly arrange a replacement, store credit, or refund.

5. Refund Processing

Once your return request has been reviewed and approved by Customer Support:

- If applicable, instructions for item drop-off or hand-delivery will be provided by our team.
- Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method or issued as store credit, depending on the transaction type and terms outlined in our Terms and Conditions.

6. Contact Us

For any questions regarding our return policies or to file a return/refund claim, please reach out to Customer Support.
